ABOUT

What is Snow flakes?

A creative project where a serialized novel and original music exist as one. Read the story and the sound feels different. Hear the music and the words carry new warmth.

📖

Novel

A completed 195-episode story following three musicians — Hinata, Kouta, and Hayate — as they navigate music, distance, and the things left unsaid. Serialized on Shōsetsuka ni Narō. Available in print (Kindle).

🎵

Music

Original songs born from within the story — streaming on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music. Each track is tied to a specific moment in the narrative.

✦

Connection

Neither the story nor the music can stand alone. A work where emotions move between the two — a new kind of experience built for readers and listeners at once.

STORY AND MUSIC

How They Work Together

"Nothing dramatic happens.
Just a brief pause, a glance away,
words left unsaid — they stay."

Snow flakes is built on a simple idea: the emotions that live between people — the ones that never get said out loud — can become music.

Each song is tied to a specific chapter. Read the scene, then hear the track. The same feeling, in two different forms. Something you couldn't quite name suddenly has a sound.

THE STORY

Hinata, Kouta, and Hayate formed a band in high school. The novel follows their lives — not the road to success, but the small shifts, the things they couldn't say, the distance that quietly moves. 195 episodes. Complete.

THE MUSIC

Original songs — composed, produced, and released on all major streaming platforms. Not background music for the novel. Not a soundtrack. Something that works alongside the story to make the emotion land differently in each form.

THE EXPERIENCE

You can start with the music and come to the novel, or start with the novel and find the music waiting. Either way, something shifts. A work where neither can stand alone — emotions move between them.

AUTHOR · COMPOSER · ILLUSTRATOR

Creator of Snow flakes — a project built around the idea that the emotions that live between people, the ones never quite put into words, can become both story and sound.

The novel ran for 195 episodes on Shōsetsuka ni Narō (Japan's largest fiction platform) and is available in print through Kindle. Original music streams worldwide on Spotify, Apple Music, and all major platforms.

Everything in Snow flakes — the writing, the music, the illustrations, the web experience — is made by one person.
